The Correspondence Desk

  • Drag any letter to shuffle the pile out of your way. When writing a reply, drag the folded letter from the desk into the open envelope.
  • Click (without dragging) a letter to open and read it — closing it sends it straight to the shredder. No re-reading.
  • Check Mail pulls in a couple of real-flavoured letters on demand; the rest arrives on its own every so often, mostly real with the occasional bit of spam.
  • Click a blank page on the desk to write by hand — select the pen, draw, flip for the back, then fold.
  • Click a blank envelope to open it, drag a folded letter inside, close the flap, address it using the on-screen keyboard, then pick up the wax seal and click the envelope's centre to seal it.
  • Drag a sealed envelope to the pigeon at the window to send it.
